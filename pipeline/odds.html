NHL Betting Information

NHL betting doesn’t get all the hype that NFL and NBA betting do, but don’t let that fool you. Just look at the 2019 Stanley Cup Playoffs, when all four No. 1 seeds failed to advance to the second round! Talk about excitement. You haven’t lived until you sweat an NHL bet.

In general, betting on the NHL is similar to betting on baseball, where moneylines and Over/Unders are most popular. Alternatively, bettors can wager on NHL puck-lines, which is the hockey version of a spread. Use our NHL odds page to compare moneylines, Over/Unders and puck-lines across online sports betting sites. You can also use our NHL odds page to find more NHL betting tips so you can join the excitement.
