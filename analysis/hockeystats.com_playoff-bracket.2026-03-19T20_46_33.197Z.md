### \*If the Playoffs Started Today

This is the playoff bracket we would see if the season ended today and the NHL used points pace for teams that have played unequal games (with tiebreakers also applied on a per-game basis), reflecting their behavior in the 2019-2020 COVID season. Probabilities shown for these matchups are the probabilities we would have if these matchups were actually taking place.

### Interactivity

To see a team's probability of further advancement, conditional on their advancement in the previous round, click on their logo in their matchup card.

### Win Probabilities

Win probabilities for these hypothetical matchups are computed via the [HockeyStats.com game simulation model](https://hockeystats.com/methodology/win-odds).

## 2025-26 NHL Playoff Bracket - First Round Matchups

Projected first round NHL playoff matchups if the season ended today:

- Colorado Avalanche vs San Jose Sharks
- Dallas Stars vs Minnesota Wild
- Carolina Hurricanes vs Boston Bruins
- Tampa Bay Lightning vs Montreal Canadiens
- Buffalo Sabres vs Columbus Blue Jackets
- Pittsburgh Penguins vs New York Islanders
- Vegas Golden Knights vs Edmonton Oilers
- Anaheim Ducks vs Utah Mammoth

Series win probabilities are calculated using 10,000 simulations per matchup, with team strength estimated from player WAR. The interactive bracket allows you to advance teams and see how probabilities update conditionally throughout the bracket.
