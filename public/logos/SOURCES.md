# Logo sources

Logos used as small visual aids next to team and league names on the player page career tables (`lib/players/logos.ts`, `lib/players/logo-registry.json`). All files are trademarks of their owners. Rasters were trimmed, fitted to 72 px and re-encoded as WebP; dark-on-transparent marks were given a light ink for the dark theme. SVGs were run through SVGO with a tight viewBox.

- NHL: era crests from the NHL standings feed (`api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/{date}` -> `teamLogo`), dark-background variants from `assets.nhle.com`. Current crests are the existing `/logos/{TRI}.svg`.
- AHL, OHL, WHL, QMJHL, USHL, ECHL, BCHL: per-season team logos from the HockeyTech/LeagueStat feeds those leagues publish (`lscluster.hockeytech.com`, `assets.leaguestat.com`).
- NCAA: ESPN team logo CDN (`a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/ncaa/500-dark/`).
- Flags: [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) 7.2.3 (MIT).
- Leagues, European clubs, junior A, prep schools: the logo in each club's or league's Wikipedia infobox (English, Russian or Finnish Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons).
